Who We Are

A Legacy of Authentic Wellness

Founded with a deep reverence for traditional healing, Bodhi Tree Foot Reflexology has been a cherished destination in Perambur for over eight years. Our name is drawn from the sacred Bodhi tree — a symbol of enlightenment, restoration, and peace — values we embody in every session we offer.

We believe that true wellness begins from the ground up. The ancient practice of foot reflexology maps the body's organ systems to precise pressure points in the feet. Our expert therapists harness this knowledge to stimulate healing, improve circulation, reduce chronic pain, and restore balance throughout the body.

Our space is designed as a refuge from the noise of modern life — warm lighting, soothing sounds, the gentle fragrance of essential oils, and a team that genuinely cares about your wellbeing. Every detail, from the organic herbal soaks to our premium massage products, is thoughtfully curated for your comfort.

Natural Pain Relief
